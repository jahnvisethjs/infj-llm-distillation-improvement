# Review scope and conservative exclusions

Policy `person1-math-review-v6` withholds multiple numbered sections without relying on a list of task verbs. Numbered givens can therefore also be withheld even when they introduce only one answer; this is a conservative review requirement, not a mathematical-error claim. The complete-source report records the actual affected counts. Forty unique previously rejected targets are mandatory canonical-hash-verified inputs, including two pair-review questions with invalid fractional vote counts.

This release preserves all original source rows and validity labels. Structural target agreement does not establish mathematical correctness. Reviews identify Codex and do not claim independent human sign-off.

Actual inspections in previous draft queues found substantive errors in Numina's `synthetic_math` and `metamath` cohorts, including invented premises, incorrect algebra and incorrect probability calculations. Both cohorts are withheld pending independent correctness review. The sample was stratified and investigated suspicious cases; it does not estimate an unbiased source error rate or establish that every excluded example is wrong. Saved case-specific findings accompany this report.

Additional complete-source filters withhold explicit multipart requests (including Roman, dollar-wrapped and escaped labels), multiple answer slots, boxed question content requiring answer-leakage review, ambiguous placeholder domains, damaged escaped indices, discrete exact answers obtained through unjustified rounding, set-parameter alternatives, and tangent questions requiring a higher derivative. These are review requirements, not proofs of incorrectness. Individual defects discovered in this release's samples are quarantined before bank allocation, with exclusions propagated to their problem groups. Previously inspected rejected targets are mandatory hash-bound inputs and cannot silently disappear when the random sample changes.

Exact matching covers the complete pinned sources. Near-instance retrieval covers the declared bounded candidate universe and supplied protected question texts, using MinHash/LSH and a second signature channel. Similarity retrieves candidates; it does not establish identity. Changed-number template relatives remain distinct. Unresolved retrieved pairs have their implicated groups withheld. Approximate retrieval can miss matches, and conservative quarantine can remove distinct problems. Detected-overlap removal does not prove zero contamination or rule out pretraining exposure.
